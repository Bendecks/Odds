# Bet365 market coverage baseline — 2026-08-30

First compact broad run queried 80 upcoming football events and retained 26,543 price observations across 120 named markets with zero Odds-API.io errors.

High-volume families included Correct Score (2,528), Player Shots (1,412), Player Shots O/U (1,412), Alternative Goal Line (964), Totals (960), Spread (728), Anytime Goalscorer (503), ML (240), Double Chance (234), Draw No Bet (160), Corners Totals (118), Both Teams To Score (52), and Bookings Totals (8).

Observation count is not evidence of value. Markets with many alternative lines or player selections naturally create many rows. The purpose of this baseline is to preserve coverage and guide later reference-model work, not to promote high-row-count markets automatically.

The immediate modelling baseline remains liquid, structurally simple markets (ML/H2H, totals and spread/handicap) while all other observations remain available for later analysis.
